The method

Three moves, no spreadsheets.

01

Trace

We read the last 24 months of transactions from your bank or card — read-only. Merchant strings like SQ *SPOTIFYUSA and ADOBE 888-… are matched against 40,000 known billers, then grouped into a single subscription with its true cadence.

  • Monthly, annual, quarterly and weekly cycles
  • Price-change history per biller
  • Free-trial → paid conversions flagged early
02

Forecast

Every subscription is projected forward, so you see the money before it leaves. A charge that lands on the 3rd and the 28th is two different decisions — we show both, plus the annual total that sneaks up on you.

  • 45-day charge calendar
  • Annualised cost of each service
  • Alerts 3 days before a renewal
03

Decide

Usage signals, price creep and duplicates become a plain-English verdict: keep it, review it, or cut it. One tap files the cancellation, and we confirm when the charges actually stop.

  • Cancel-links for 900+ services
  • Downgrade paths before full cancellation
  • We verify the charge stopped

Cross-examination

Questions, answered plainly.

How do you find subscriptions I've forgotten?

We read the last 24 months of transactions from a connected bank or card account (read-only). Recurring charges are identified by merchant, cadence, amount stability and date rhythm. Anything that repeats on a schedule — monthly, quarterly, annual, weekly — is surfaced as a subscription with its full history.

Do you need my bank password?

Never. We connect through licensed open-banking aggregators, which means you authorise read-only access inside your own bank's interface. We never see, transmit or store your credentials, and you can revoke the connection at any time from the app or your bank.

Can Duespy cancel subscriptions for me?

For over 900 services we hold the direct cancellation link, and one tap files the request. For services that require a phone call or an email, we prepare the wording and track whether the charge actually stops. Either way, we verify the outcome against your statements.

What if I genuinely use the subscription?

Then keep it — that's the point. Duespy is not a tool for cancelling things. It's a tool for knowing what you own. Plenty of users keep everything and simply switch to the annual plan, or move to a cheaper tier, once they can see the real cost.

Is my data sold or used for ads?

No. Your data is not sold, rented or used for advertising, and we don't run ads. Our revenue comes only from subscriptions, which is exactly why the product has to be honest with you about what to cut.

Which countries are supported?

We currently support banks in the US, UK, Canada, Australia and the EU. Prices are shown in your local currency, and cancellation links are region-aware. More markets are being added each quarter.
